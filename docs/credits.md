## Services

Photon is operated by komoot, the OSRM routing servers by FOSSGIS, OpenRouteService by HeiGIT, and the
public Nominatim and Overpass instances by the OpenStreetMap community. Travya caches their answers and
respects their usage policies.

## Software

Travya is built on open-source software, among others FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLModel, SQLAlchemy,
Alembic, Google ADK, pgvector, React, Vite, TanStack Router and Query, Chakra UI, MapLibre GL and
MkDocs Material.
